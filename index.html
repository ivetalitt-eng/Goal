Unprecedented Results

Results That
Redefine What's Possible

In Phase 2 clinical trials, retatrutide demonstrated weight loss outcomes that surpassed all previously approved anti-obesity medications.

  • Up to 24.2% mean body weight reduction over 48 weeks
  • 100% of participants at highest dose achieved ≥5% weight loss
  • 83% of participants achieved ≥15% weight loss (vs. 0% on placebo)
  • Significant improvements in cardiometabolic markers
  • Favorable safety profile consistent with GLP-1 class
  • Once-weekly subcutaneous injection for convenience

Retatrutide is a triple agonist that activates GLP-1, GIP, and glucagon receptors simultaneously. Semaglutide (Ozempic/Wegovy) targets only GLP-1. The addition of GIP and especially glucagon receptor activity substantially increases energy expenditure and fat oxidation, which explains the superior weight loss observed in trials.
Retatrutide is currently in Phase 3 clinical trials and has not yet received FDA approval for obesity treatment. Our program provides access under physician oversight as part of monitored early-access protocols. Full FDA review is expected following Phase 3 completion.
The most common side effects in Phase 2 trials were gastrointestinal — nausea (45%), vomiting (22%), and diarrhea (20%) — most common during dose escalation and typically resolving within 2–4 weeks. The side effect profile is consistent with the GLP-1 medication class. Serious adverse events were rare and similar to placebo.
Retatrutide is administered as a once-weekly subcutaneous injection, similar to semaglutide and tirzepatide. Dosing begins low and is escalated gradually over several months to minimize gastrointestinal side effects and optimize tolerability.
Eligibility requires adults aged 18–70 with a BMI ≥30 (or ≥27 with at least one weight-related comorbidity), no personal/family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN2, no current pregnancy, and no severe gastrointestinal disease. Final eligibility is determined by a physician review after application.
Most participants begin experiencing appetite reduction within 2–4 weeks of starting treatment. Meaningful weight loss (5%+) is typically observed within 8–12 weeks. The most significant body weight reduction occurs between weeks 24–48 as the therapeutic dose is reached and maintained.
